Product Philosophy

Every image has to get past us first.

Most AI staging ships whatever the image model returns. Ours clears seven gates first — two of them AI reviewers whose only job is to say no.

1

Bounded Request

Transformation, style and room type from a closed menu. Furnishing notes are translated, never handed to the model.

2

Room Plan

We measure the room first: doors, windows, switches, vents and every fixed feature that must survive the edit.

3

Generation

Several candidates are rendered in parallel, each inside the same structural guardrails for that room.

4

AI Compliance Review

An AI vision model inspects the result against a written rulebook — three independent passes, majority decides.

5

AI Realism Review

A second vision model zooms into artwork, mirrors and rugs, hunting the flaws that give AI staging away.

6

Disclosure

A required disclosure watermark is applied to the finished image automatically — it cannot be turned off.

7

Delivery

Only an image that clears all seven gates is released. Anything else is rejected and the credit returned.

What the compliance model checks

Architecture, framing and camera unchanged. Nothing invented — no new windows, fixtures or light sources. Furniture scaled to the room. Doors that still open.

What the realism model checks

The tells buyers spot up close: smeared artwork, melted furniture, mirrors reflecting a room that isn't there, floating rugs, trim eaten away behind a sofa.

Who sets the standard

Reviews are automatic. The standard is human — hundreds of real listing results, each passed or failed by the photographer who built this. When one slips through, the rule changes.
