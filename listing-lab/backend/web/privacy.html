Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Listing Lab is a product of Horizon Home Media, based in Massachusetts, USA. This policy says what we collect, what happens to your photographs, and what we never do with either. It is written to be read.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash — we cannot read it, and neither can anyone who steals a copy of our database.
  • Your photographs and the finished results, stored so we can process and deliver your work.
  • Job records: which transformation ran, when, its cost in credits, and the automated quality checks it passed — the receipt for your work.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. Card numbers never touch our servers; we receive only confirmation that a purchase happened, for how much, and for which credit pack.

We use one session cookie to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly and used for nothing else. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

What happens to your photos

To perform a transformation, your photo is sent to our AI processing providers — currently Google's Gemini image services — and the generated result comes back to us for automated compliance review before delivery. Photos and results are stored in our cloud storage (Cloudflare) so you can retrieve your work. Your originals are never modified; every edit is a new file.

We never sell your photographs, never use them for advertising, and never show them to other customers. They are processed by automated systems; our staff access them only when needed to support you or investigate a fault.

Who we share with

  • Google — AI image processing, as described above.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, storage, and delivery infrastructure.
  • Stripe — payment processing.

Each receives only what it needs to do its job. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, and we disclose it beyond these providers only if the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your photos and results while your account is active so your work stays available to you. To have them removed, ask us — through the support box in the app or at support@thelistinglab.app — and we erase your photos, results and account details from our storage within 30 days. The only records we keep are the minimal purchase records we are required to retain (such as credit purchases and receipts).

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are salted and hashed; session tokens are stored only as hashes; payment credentials are never in our possession at all. No system is perfect, but the design assumes a stolen database must not become stolen accounts.

Your choices

You can view your work and buy or spend credits from within the app. For deletion of your photos or account, data questions, or a copy of your data, contact us and we will handle it promptly. If you are in a jurisdiction that grants specific privacy rights (such as access, correction, or deletion), we honor them.

Children

The Service is for professionals and is not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so on this page with an updated date.
